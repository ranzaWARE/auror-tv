AUROR TV

Glasshouse Home, in a browser

The home screen AUROR puts on an LG TV, running here with made-up apps, inputs and shows. Settings change as they would on the TV and last until the page is closed; nothing opens, since there is no TV behind it.

Keys

ArrowsMove, as the remote's arrows do. Up from the first row reaches Search, the network, the sleep timer, the theme, Settings and switching off.
EnterOK. Held for a second, it arranges the tiles.
Esc or BackspaceBack: Recent apps from the rows, or out of a panel.
MouseThe Magic Remote's pointer: hover, click, the wheel, and resting at a row's end glides it.

The view is the one in this repository's server, built into this site on each change; the TV's answers come from a small stand-in in the page.
